# AegisCare brand direction

The public landing page uses a warm, editorial palette drawn from the supplied reference image, with a distinct healthcare-specific visual story.

## Palette

- Forest: `#3D574A`
- Cream: `#F4ECDF`
- Coral: `#F88A79`
- Supporting sage: `#DCE4D8`
- Deep ink: `#263D33`

## Design choices

- No stock photography or externally hosted hero image.
- The hero artwork is original inline SVG: one continuous line joining three care moments.
- Display headings use Newsreader with DM Sans for interface and body copy.
- Coral is used as a deliberate accent, not as an emergency/severity color.
- Copy describes the product's intended coordination role and avoids implying that it diagnoses or replaces clinical judgement.
